01 · ABOUT ME

The story behind
the work.

I’ve always enjoyed building teams, creating experiences, and making work run better.

I started in HR during undergrad, helping grow an edtech startup. I loved the work: curating experiences, building teams, and creating processes that helped people thrive.

The summer after graduation, I gave teaching a shot—teaching business in China and bridging cultures between two countries. I carried that same passion for people and learning into larger organizations, building teams and processes at scale. At Wattpad / Webtoon Studios, that grew into people operations, acquisition integration, and company-wide OKRs across a distributed team.

Consulting at Carpedia and Adaptovate let me work on both business operations and AI adoption. At Carpedia, I delivered $10M in realized annual savings and built playbooks to standardize work and reduce costs for the business. At Adaptovate, I helped teams develop prompt-engineering skills and, as a Scrum Master, adopt agile ways of working.

My MBA at USC Marshall brought me back to something I’ve always loved: education. Alongside serving as Class President, I co-designed the first AI core curriculum for incoming MBAs. I wanted students to leave with skills they could actually use.

These days, I’m working with Diaspora Health’s founders on an AI-native behavioural health platform that helps patients make sense of their insurance coverage and what they can expect to pay. Getting care shouldn’t mean guessing what it will cost.
